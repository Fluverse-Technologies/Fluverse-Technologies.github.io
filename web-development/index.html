Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a business website?

It depends on the number of pages, features and how quickly content and feedback are ready. A simple company website is much quicker than a custom web application, and we share a clear timeline along with the quote.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and SEO-ready?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first and includes titles, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, an XML sitemap, structured data and analytics as standard.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We review your current site and rankings first so the redesign keeps the pages and URLs that already bring traffic, and sets up redirects where needed.

Do you also build web applications and portals?

Yes. We build dashboards, admin panels, customer portals and workflow tools connected to your APIs and databases.

Do you provide hosting and maintenance?

We can set up domain and hosting for you and offer ongoing maintenance for content updates, fixes and improvements.
