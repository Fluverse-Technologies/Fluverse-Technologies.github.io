Target links that have a genuine editorial/business reason: client case studies, technology partner pages, local business associations, event/sponsorship pages, founder interviews, useful engineering articles, supplier/partner profiles and reputable startup/business publications. ## 5. Local authority content
Publish proof-driven content, not cloned city pages. Each should contain original experience, screenshots/data where permission exists, and a clear enquiry CTA.

## 6. Reviews
After real project milestones, ask clients for honest Google reviews. Never offer incentives for positive reviews. Reply professionally and naturally. Reviews should reflect actual services and experience.

## 7. Monthly measurement
Google Search Console: clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, indexed pages, queries by service/location. GA4: enquiry-email clicks, form submissions (when a working form is connected), phone/WhatsApp clicks if added later. Google Business Profile: calls, website clicks, direction requests and search terms where available.
