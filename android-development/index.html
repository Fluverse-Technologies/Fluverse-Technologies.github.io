Should I build Android first or both Android and iOS?

If most of your users are in India, launching on Android first is often the most cost-effective start. With Flutter, adding iOS later reuses most of the code.

Can you publish the app on Google Play for us?

Yes. We prepare the release build, store listing, screenshots, privacy policy and data-safety declarations and manage the Play Console submission and review.

Can the app work offline?

Yes. Data can be stored on the device and synced when a connection is available, which is important for field staff and factory floors.

Technology focus: Flutter, Dart, Kotlin platform channels, Firebase, Firebase Cloud Messaging, REST APIs, UPI and Razorpay, Google Maps, offline storage, Play Console and staged releases.
