Why businesses choose Flutter

A single Dart codebase ships to Android and iOS, so features, fixes and design changes reach both platforms together. For most business apps this cuts build time and maintenance cost significantly compared with two native teams.

MVP in weeks, not months

We scope a focused first version around your core user journey, launch it on the Play Store and App Store, then add features based on real usage instead of assumptions.

Rebuilding an existing app

Ageing native, hybrid or poorly structured Flutter apps can be audited and migrated in phases, keeping the live app running while modules are replaced.

Backend, payments & integrations

Firebase, custom REST APIs, Razorpay and other payment gateways, maps, push notifications, OTP login and third-party SDKs are planned as part of the architecture, not bolted on later.

Maintainable architecture

BLoC or Provider state management, Clean Architecture layers and reusable widget libraries keep the app easy for your team, or ours, to extend for years.

Testing, release & support

Unit and widget tests, CI/CD with GitHub Actions or Fastlane, store submission and post-launch monitoring, with an optional monthly support plan.

How much does a Flutter app cost in Silvassa or Gujarat?

It depends on the number of screens, integrations and user roles. A focused MVP is usually far cheaper than two separate native apps because one codebase serves Android and iOS. We share a written estimate after a free discovery call.

How long does it take to build a Flutter app?

It depends on scope. A focused MVP with one user role is much quicker than a platform with admin panels and multiple user roles. After the discovery call we share a milestone timeline with a release date for each phase.
